🌱 SmartAgro– Smart Crop Disease & Pest Management System
Early detection and management of crop diseases and pest infestations using AI, weather data, and smart agricultural insights.
📌 About the Project
AgriTwin is an AI-powered smart farming platform designed to help farmers detect crop diseases and pest infestations at an early stage and make better farming decisions.
The system combines AI-based analysis, weather information, crop health monitoring, water optimization, and farmer-friendly recommendations in one platform.
🎯 Problem Statement
Farmers often face crop losses due to:
🌾 Crop diseases
🐛 Pest infestations
🌦️ Unexpected weather conditions
💧 Improper water usage
📊 Lack of timely agricultural information
💰 Uncertain crop profitability
AgriTwin provides timely, data-based insights to reduce these risks.
🚀 Key Features
🤖 AI-Based Disease Detection
Detect possible crop diseases using crop images and provide early alerts.
🐛 Pest Identification
Identify common pests and provide suitable management suggestions.
🌦️ Weather-Based Risk Prediction
Analyze weather conditions to identify possible disease and pest risks.
💧 AI Water Optimizer
Provide smart irrigation recommendations based on crop and environmental conditions.
🛰️ Satellite Crop Health Map
Monitor crop health using satellite-based information.
📈 Future Farm Prediction
Predict possible crop health and farming conditions.
💰 Profit Predictor
Estimate potential crop profitability based on farming-related data.
🚨 Farm Risk Radar
Display major risks affecting the farm and provide early warnings.
🗣️ Voice + Local Language AI
Provide farmer assistance through Tamil, English, and Malayalam.
🛒 Smart Crop Marketplace
Help farmers explore crop/market-related opportunities.
🏆 Farm Decision Score
Provide an overall score to help farmers understand their current farming situation.
